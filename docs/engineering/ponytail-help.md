## What it does

`ponytail-help` is a quick-reference card for the whole ponytail suite: its three intensity levels and its six skills, with what each one does and how to trigger it.

## When to reach for it

Type `/ponytail-help`, or ask "what ponytail commands" or "how do I use ponytail."

## It's working if

You can find the one command you needed without opening any other skill's `SKILL.md`.

## Where it fits

A one-shot display, the index over [ponytail](https://aihero.dev/skills-ponytail), [ponytail-review](https://aihero.dev/skills-ponytail-review), [ponytail-audit](https://aihero.dev/skills-ponytail-audit), [ponytail-debt](https://aihero.dev/skills-ponytail-debt), and [ponytail-gain](https://aihero.dev/skills-ponytail-gain). For the whole repo's skills, not just this suite, [ask-matt](https://aihero.dev/skills-ask-matt) is the broader router.
